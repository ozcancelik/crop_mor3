Crop Mor3 (5D Mark III)
=======================

High-resolution 1:1 crop, 1x3 tall anamorphic (up to 5.4K/5.7K) and 3x3
High FPS LiveView preview and RAW readout modes for Canon EOS 5D Mark III
(1.1.3 & 1.2.3) with real-time hardware WYSIWYG LiveView preview.

No ROM, NVRAM or Canon property writes.
