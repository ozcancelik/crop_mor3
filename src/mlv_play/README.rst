MLV Player
==========

Plays Magic Lantern video files (both MLV and RAW).
Press PLAY in LV to open the gallery,
or select a clip for playback in the file_man module.

Crop Mor3 build: 3x2 gallery with thumbnails and clip details,
.PRX proxies for real-time playback with sound, superpixel
renderer (10/12/14-bit, LJ92), LUTs and .cube files (ML/LUTS),
automatic Bayer phase and 1x3 desqueeze.

* "all": play every video frame
* "exact": drop video frames to match recorded FPS

Gallery keys
------------

* SET: play the selected clip
* INFO: clip details
* TRASH: delete (starts on Cancel)
* MENU or PLAY: exit

Playback keys
-------------

* SET or Q: use the on-screen menu
* PLAY: pause or resume playback
* wheel: switch to previous or next video
* RATE: next LUT
* ZOOM: zoom in, arrows to pan
* INFO: toggle display of video information
* MENU: back to the gallery
